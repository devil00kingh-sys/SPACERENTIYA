1. About SpaceRentiya

Welcome to SpaceRentiya (“SpaceRentiya”, “we”, “us”, or “our”).

SpaceRentiya is a real-estate services and advisory company providing property-related services and connecting clients with selected real-estate opportunities across India, Dubai/UAE and Singapore.

Our services may include residential and commercial real estate, property sales, leasing and rentals, property acquisition assistance, property advisory, real-estate investment opportunities, property sourcing, developer and owner introductions, tenant and landlord services, commercial property services, transaction coordination, real-estate consulting, property-related documentation and support, and other real-estate services.

This Privacy Policy explains how SpaceRentiya collects, uses, stores, protects and shares personal information when you use our website, contact us, enquire about a property, request our services, make or receive a payment through our services, or otherwise interact with us.

2. Information We Collect

Personal Information: Full name, date of birth where required, email, mobile number, address, nationality, country of residence, identification information, company/organisation name, designation, professional information and communication preferences.

Property Information: Property requirements, preferred location, property type, purchase/rental requirements, budget range, investment preferences, transaction requirements, ownership information, tenant/landlord information, developer information and property documents supplied by you.

Technical Information: IP address, browser and device information, operating system, pages visited, date/time of access, referring website and interaction information.

3. KYC and Verification Information

For certain transactions or services, SpaceRentiya may conduct identity, compliance or transaction-related verification. Depending on the transaction, we may request government-issued identification, PAN or equivalent tax identification, passport details, address verification, company registration information, corporate documents, ownership documents, authorisation documents, source-of-funds information where legally or contractually required, and other documents required by applicable law or transaction partners.

4. Payment Information

SpaceRentiya may process information relating to payments for its services and transactions, including payment amount, transaction reference number, payment date/time, payment status, invoice and billing information, payment-provider information, refund information, commission, service charges, booking amounts, security deposits where applicable, and other transaction records.

Where payments are processed through a third-party payment gateway, bank or financial institution, payment information may be processed directly by that provider under its own terms and privacy policy.

SpaceRentiya does not necessarily store complete debit-card, credit-card or banking credentials on its own systems. Where a third-party payment processor is used, sensitive payment credentials may be handled directly by the payment processor.

5. How We Use Your Information

  • Respond to property enquiries and identify suitable properties.
  • Provide property advisory services and arrange property viewings.
  • Facilitate introductions to property owners, developers and transaction partners.
  • Assist with sales, purchases, leasing and rentals.
  • Manage client relationships, proposals, quotations, invoices and payments.
  • Verify transactions and conduct KYC or compliance checks.
  • Calculate commissions and service fees and maintain transaction records.
  • Provide customer support and schedule meetings.
  • Improve our website and services, maintain security and analyse website usage.
  • Comply with applicable laws and regulatory requirements and resolve disputes.

6. Property Owners, Developers and Real-Estate Partners

SpaceRentiya may work with property owners, developers, landlords, tenants, buyers, sellers, real-estate brokers, agents, property managers, legal professionals, financial institutions, valuation professionals, consultants and other transaction-related service providers.

Where necessary to fulfil your request, relevant information may be shared with the appropriate party. For example, a property enquiry may require us to provide your contact details and enquiry information to the relevant owner, developer, broker or authorised representative.

7. Property Transactions

SpaceRentiya may facilitate or coordinate certain aspects of a property transaction. Depending on the specific transaction, SpaceRentiya may not be the property owner, developer, landlord, tenant, buyer, seller, lender, legal representative or payment processor.

Property-specific terms, transaction agreements, booking forms, engagement letters, invoices, brokerage agreements and other contractual documents may contain additional terms applicable to a particular transaction.

8. Fees, Commissions and Charges

Depending on the service provided, SpaceRentiya may receive or charge advisory fees, brokerage fees, service fees, consultation fees, transaction fees, property sourcing fees, referral fees, commission or other agreed charges.

The applicable amount, payment schedule and responsibility for payment will be communicated through the relevant quotation, proposal, invoice, engagement agreement or transaction document.

This Privacy Policy does not itself create a payment obligation. Any payment obligation will arise only where agreed through applicable commercial or transaction documentation.

9. Refunds and Payment Disputes

Where a refund is applicable, the applicable refund terms will depend on the service purchased, applicable agreement, property transaction, payment provider, reason for refund and applicable law.

Refunds may be processed through the original payment method where technically and legally possible. Payment disputes should be reported to SpaceRentiya as soon as reasonably possible with the relevant transaction details.

10. International Transactions and Data Processing

SpaceRentiya may provide services relating to properties and transactions in India, Dubai/UAE and Singapore. Personal information may therefore be processed or transferred across jurisdictions where necessary to provide requested services or complete a transaction.

Different countries may have different privacy and data-protection requirements. SpaceRentiya will take reasonable measures appropriate to applicable legal and operational requirements.

11. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website may use cookies, analytics technologies, session technologies, security technologies, performance monitoring tools and similar technologies to operate the website, remember preferences, understand visitor behaviour, improve performance, detect security issues and measure traffic.

You may control certain cookies through your browser settings.

12. Sharing of Personal Information

SpaceRentiya may share personal information where reasonably necessary with property owners, developers, buyers, sellers, landlords, tenants, brokers, agents, property managers, legal and professional advisors, banks and financial institutions, payment processors, accounting providers, technology and hosting providers, CRM and communication providers, analytics providers, compliance providers, government authorities, regulators and law-enforcement authorities where legally required.

We do not intend to sell your personal information as an independent commercial product.

13. Legal and Regulatory Disclosure

SpaceRentiya may disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to lawful government requests, meet regulatory requirements, prevent fraud, investigate unlawful activity, protect the rights and property of SpaceRentiya, protect clients and users, enforce contracts and resolve legal disputes.

14. Data Security

SpaceRentiya uses reasonable technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, loss, misuse, alteration and destruction. However, no website, electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

15. Data Retention

We may retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for providing services, maintaining client relationships, completing transactions, accounting and financial records, tax requirements, legal and regulatory compliance, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, contract enforcement and business recordkeeping.

When information is no longer required, we may delete, anonymise or securely dispose of it subject to applicable legal requirements.

16. Your Data Protection Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including access to relevant information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion where legally applicable, withdrawal of consent where processing is based on consent, and the ability to raise privacy-related complaints.

India’s Digital Personal Data Protection framework provides mechanisms concerning personal-data processing and individual rights, subject to the applicable commencement and rules.

17. Marketing Communications

With appropriate consent or where otherwise permitted by applicable law, SpaceRentiya may send information about new properties, property launches, real-estate opportunities, market updates, services, events and company announcements.

You may request to stop receiving marketing communications. Essential service-related communications concerning an existing enquiry, transaction, payment, appointment or contractual relationship may still be sent.

18. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, property portals, payment providers, developers, financial institutions or other services. SpaceRentiya does not control the privacy practices of third-party websites. You should review their respective privacy policies before providing personal information.

19. Children’s Privacy

SpaceRentiya’s services are intended primarily for adults and business users. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in circumstances where such collection is prohibited by applicable law.

20. Fraud and Security

SpaceRentiya may use information to detect and prevent fraudulent transactions, fake property enquiries, identity misuse, payment fraud, account misuse, unauthorised access and other unlawful or harmful activity. We may suspend or restrict access to services where reasonably necessary to protect users, transactions or the platform.

21. Changes to This Privacy Policy

SpaceRentiya may update this Privacy Policy from time to time due to changes in our business, services, technology, applicable laws, data-processing practices or regulatory requirements. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised Last Updated date.

22. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy shall be interpreted in accordance with applicable laws. For personal-data processing connected with India, applicable Indian data-protection laws and regulations may apply. For services or transactions involving other jurisdictions, additional local laws and contractual requirements may apply.
